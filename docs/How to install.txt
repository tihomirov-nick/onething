ONE THING
=========

INSTALLING
Open One Thing right from this window. It moves itself to the Applications
folder and restarts from there. Without administrator rights, One Thing goes
to the Applications folder inside your home folder. Once it runs, its icon
appears in the menu bar at the top right of the screen.

You can also install One Thing by hand. Drag its icon onto the Applications
folder next to it and launch One Thing from the Applications folder.

IF macOS WON'T OPEN THE APP
The app isn't notarized by Apple, so on the first launch macOS may show
a warning ("cannot verify the developer" or "Apple could not verify it is free
of malware"). You only need to allow it once.

  • On macOS 15 and later, close the warning with "Done", open System
    Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
    next to One Thing and confirm.

  • On macOS 14, right-click One Thing in Finder, choose "Open", then click
    "Open" again in the warning.

  • If that didn't help, drag One Thing onto the Applications folder, open
    Terminal and run:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/One Thing.app"

THE PHRASE IN THE MENU BAR
One Thing shows one phrase in the menu bar: the main thing for today. While
there is no phrase, a small mark, an opening quotation mark, stands in its
place. A click on the icon or the phrase opens a panel with a field. Return
saves the phrase, Esc closes the panel without changes. An empty phrase brings
the mark back. A right click or a Control-click opens the menu: settings,
checking for updates, About and Quit.

PHRASE SETTINGS
In Settings (⌘,), in the "Phrase" section, you can set the maximum width of
the text in the menu bar, hide the text and keep only the mark, choose a font
and its style, and record a shortcut for editing. A long phrase is cut with an
ellipsis, the whole phrase shows in the tooltip of the icon. Any installed font
will do: the list has a search field, and the field in the panel shows the
phrase in the same font. If the font disappears from the Mac, One Thing goes
back to the system font. The shortcut opens the field from any app. At first
there is none: click "Not set", press a shortcut, Esc cancels, ⌫ removes it.

LANGUAGE
One Thing works in Russian and English. In Settings (⌘,) choose "Same as
System", "Русский" or "English". With "Same as System" One Thing takes the
first suitable language from the list in System Settings > General >
Language & Region. A new language takes effect after a restart, and the
"Restart" button appears right under the choice.

UPDATES
One Thing looks for a new version on GitHub at launch, every three hours and
after the Mac wakes up. It downloads a new version by itself, checks its
signature and installs it when One Thing is idle. With "Install updates
automatically" turned off in Settings, the icon gets a dot instead, and
One Thing's panel shows a card with "Later" and "Update".

OPEN AT LOGIN
One Thing opens at login to install updates on time. To stop it, turn off
"Open at login" in Settings.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 14 or later, a Mac with Apple Silicon or Intel.
